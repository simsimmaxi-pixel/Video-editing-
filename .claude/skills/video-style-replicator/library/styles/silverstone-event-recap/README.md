# Style profile: social event recap, burst/hold montage (vertical)

**Source reference:** a 21.3 s TikTok recap of a padel-club social (Northampton), 9:16 at 30 fps.
**Recreated as:** three approved Silverstone recaps (v1 with a caption, v1-extended to the song's end, v2 from the song's top).
Full measured breakdown: `reference-blueprint.md`. Edit files: `edits/`.

## When this profile fits
Event, party, venue or community recaps; "you missed this" FOMO posts; any montage of many short phone or
gimbal clips over one song, with people as the subject.

## The grammar in numbers
- 38 shots in 21.2 s. ASD 0.56 s, but **bimodal**: bursts at 0.33-0.35 s (6-12 f) alternate with holds at 1.4-2.0 s (45-61 f).
- Blocks as a fraction of runtime: burst 0-0.28 | hold 0.28-0.55 | burst 0.55-0.76 | hold 0.76-0.90 | outro 0.90-1.0.
- Hard cuts only. Energy comes from in-camera motion (walk-ins, whips, a rotated selfie). 3 black frames at the end.
- Cuts **not** beat-synced (mean offset 107 ms vs 99 ms random); blocks follow the song's sections (C01).
- Bursts rotate venue → person → activity. Holds are only for human payoff (dance, tasting reaction, selfie, high-five).
- Natural phone look, no grade: L50 ≈ 48, sat ≈ 0.29, slightly warm.
- One persistent location caption, centred at y = 0.50, 3.4% of frame height. It was dropped from later versions at the user's request.

## What the user approved and asked to change
- Liked: the hook (eye contact to the lens, already moving), the hold on the music's break with the payoff on its
  re-entry (M02), the payoff selfie hold, and the bookend venue ending.
- Changed: the venue-sign whip was 12 f, which is too short to read, so it became 36 f (1.2 s), giving the
  general rule now in the engine's `text=` check. The caption was removed in later versions. Videos extended to
  the song's real end (M03). A second video used the song from its top (M04) with different hooks and holds.

## Techniques used
P01 P02 P03 P04 · C01 C03 · T01 T02 · R01 · M01 M02 M03 M04 · S05 · X01 (v1 only)
